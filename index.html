Built around the passenger

Alpha Airways was founded in 2009 by a small group of airline veterans who believed flying should be simple. Clear prices, honest timekeeping and real help when plans change.

Today we are an independent airline based in Indianapolis, with thousands of people working across our operations, maintenance and cabin crews.

Every decision starts with one question: would we want this on our own flight?
